# Community Code of Conduct

LevelUp Architect is a learning community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to create an environment where people can experiment, ask basic questions, challenge ideas, and learn without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique code, architecture, or content rather than the person.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and learning styles.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing credentials or learner data.
- Attribute sources and respect licenses, trademarks, and certification confidentiality.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and help keep discussions focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate humiliation, sustained disruption, or publishing another person’s private information is not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies to repository interactions, project events, and public representation of the project.
